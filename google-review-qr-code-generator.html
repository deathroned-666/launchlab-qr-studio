Google Review QR Code Generator

Create a QR code that sends happy customers to your Google review page. It works well on receipts, table tents, checkout counters, thank-you cards, product inserts, and service follow-up flyers.

Create a Google review QR code

How to create one

  1. Open your Google Business Profile and copy the direct review link.
  2. Paste the link into URL mode in the LaunchLab QR Studio generator.
  3. Use a short label such as "Review us on Google" near the printed code.
  4. Download PNG for office printing or SVG for a designer or print shop.
  5. Scan the final printed proof before placing it in front of customers.

Trust and policy notes

Ask for honest feedback instead of promising rewards for positive reviews. Keep the printed request simple and avoid wording that pressures the customer. A useful review QR code should reduce friction, not manipulate the review.

Print tips

For receipts, keep the code at least 1 inch wide. For table signs or counters, 1.6 to 2 inches is easier to scan. Use dark modules on a light background, leave quiet space around the QR code, and test from the same distance customers will use.
