vCard QR Code Generator

A vCard QR code lets someone save your contact details after scanning your business card, badge, sign, or flyer.

Create a vCard QR code

Best fields to include

  • Full name and company name.
  • Mobile phone or office phone.
  • Email address and website.
  • Only the fields people actually need, because shorter QR codes scan faster.

Business card advice

Use SVG for professional print layouts, keep the QR code at least 0.8 inches wide, and avoid placing it too close to the trim edge.
