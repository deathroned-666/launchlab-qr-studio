Subscriber dashboard

Manage dynamic QR destinations after printing.

Claim your PayPal subscription, create a managed short QR URL, print that QR code, then update its destination any time from this dashboard.

How it works
  1. Subscribe with PayPal and copy your subscription ID.
  2. Unlock this dashboard with your PayPal email.
  3. Create a dynamic QR and print the LaunchLab redirect QR.

Claim subscriber access

Use the email on your PayPal subscription. This keeps casual static QR users separate from paid dynamic QR management.

After subscribing, PayPal sends LaunchLab a webhook. If your subscription is brand new, wait a minute before claiming access.
Where is the subscription ID? It appears after PayPal approval and in PayPal subscription emails. If you opened this page from the pricing success link, LaunchLab will fill it in automatically.

Create dynamic QR

The printed QR should point to the LaunchLab redirect URL. You can change the destination later without reprinting.

Brand the downloaded QR

These print settings stay in this browser. Your logo image is not uploaded or saved on LaunchLab servers.
