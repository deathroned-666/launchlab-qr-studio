QR Code Size Guide

The right QR size depends on scan distance, print quality, contrast, and how much data is inside the code. Larger is safer, especially for posters and table signs.

Use the size calculator

Print useStarting sizeNotes
Business card0.8 in / 20 mmUse a short URL and keep margin around the code.
Flyer1.2 in / 30 mmGood for hand-held scanning.
Restaurant table sign1.6 in / 40 mmTest under room lighting and avoid glare.
Poster4 in / 100 mmIncrease size as viewing distance grows.

Quick rule

Start with a QR width around one-tenth of the expected scanning distance, then test the final printed material.
