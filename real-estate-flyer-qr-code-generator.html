  • Listing page with photos, price, beds, baths, and contact details.
  • Virtual tour or video walkthrough.

  • Google Maps location for land, rentals, or new developments.

Static vs dynamic for listings

Use a free static QR code for a stable listing URL. Use dynamic QR when flyers may stay in circulation after the destination changes, such as sold listings, price updates, open house times, or rotating lead forms.

Scan confidence

Real estate flyers often use photos and colored backgrounds. Keep the QR code on a clean light block with high contrast. Avoid placing it over property photos, textured gradients, or glossy dark areas.
