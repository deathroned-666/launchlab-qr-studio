Batch preparation tips

  • Use one destination per line.
  • Keep URLs short when possible.
  • Export the CSV to keep a record of labels and destinations.
  • Test a sample before printing a full batch.

Local workflow

The batch preview runs in the browser. For larger production batches, keep a spreadsheet with destination, label, campaign, print size, and proof status.
