Print tips for stationery

  • Use a simple dark QR code on a light background.
  • Avoid placing the code over textured artwork or heavy floral patterns.
  • Keep the code at least 1 inch wide on invitation inserts.
  • Print a physical proof and test it before ordering a full batch.

When to use dynamic QR

If the RSVP link might change after invitations are mailed, use a dynamic QR plan. That lets you update the destination without reprinting or contacting every guest with a corrected URL.
