About LaunchLab QR Studio

LaunchLab QR Studio helps people create reliable static QR codes for real-world uses: menus, posters, business cards, Wi-Fi signs, event handouts, and contact sharing.

Why this site exists

Many QR tools make users wonder whether a free QR code will expire or whether a printed code depends on a paid account. This site focuses on static QR codes: codes that contain the destination directly and keep working after download.

What makes it useful

  • Multiple QR formats in one place.
  • Clear print-size guidance for common use cases.
  • Scannability warnings for contrast and dense payloads.
  • Fast downloads in SVG and PNG formats.
